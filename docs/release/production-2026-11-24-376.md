# Production release 2026-11-24: Leaderboard UI and counting (`20261130001300`, #376)

Released under the founder's 2026-11-24 "Comments Polish, QA Closeout & Fast Production Release" approval. That approval authorises this one vetted migration for production once the gates pass, and nothing else from staging. It is released on its own, independently of the Comments work in #374.

## Change
[#376](https://github.com/fourwardhabits/bingd/pull/376), squash-merged as `9fdd7026`.

- **Header.** LEADERBOARD sits beside the Feed / Trophy / People switch. Under it is one row with a quiet category dropdown (All Titles, Movies, TV Shows, Reviews) and an **All time | This month** switch. Both choices are remembered per account.
- **Counting.** `_leaderboard_counts` now counts each watched title once:
  - All Titles = movies + whole shows + seasons;
  - TV Shows = whole shows + seasons;
  - **This month** needs a native-dated watch event in the UTC month;
  - **All time** keeps the import rule.
  - Reviews is unchanged.
  - Callers, signatures, grants, population, ties and limits are unchanged.
- **Codex:** clean on `65d3b59d`.
- **Native build:** none needed. The change is JS plus one migration, and the fingerprint is unchanged (Android `f4062003…`, iOS `bf0ce597…`).

## OTA 6 (`03b4769d`: [#374](https://github.com/fourwardhabits/bingd/pull/374), Comments)
Released on its own, after #376, under the same approval. It is JS-only: no migration, no edge function, and no native change.

**What it ships**
- A notification opens a reply to that comment, in a focused thread with "See all N comments".
- The reply indicator is just "Replying to {Name}".
- The post appears as the Feed's card, with a compact Like and reaction picker on the Comments page. It uses the same mutation and query keys as the Feed.
- The Feed's comment sheet opens at its content height, swipes up to 85% and down to close, and has a Close button. Unsent drafts are kept in memory for each post.
- Copy cleanup for comment states, the Comments notification setting and reply notifications. The preference semantics are unchanged.
