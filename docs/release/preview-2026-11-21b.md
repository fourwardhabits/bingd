## Founder Preview QA (Android first, then iOS)

For #322, use three accounts: an author A, a follower F who follows A, and a person B whom A follows.

**#322: follow stories**
1. On F's Feed, A's follow story shows **Like · Comment**. A tap reacts, a long press opens the picker, and "… like this" opens who reacted.
2. Comment opens the shared sheet with no "Contains spoilers" toggle. A ranking post still has it.
3. A is notified "reacted to / commented on your follow activity", in the inbox and as a push, and a tap opens the activity page. B gets nothing.
4. After a reaction, a new follow by A makes a **new** story, even if the reaction is removed.
5. A unfollows B, the only member: the conversation disappears for F and A. A re-follows B, and it returns.
6. At the largest text size on a narrow phone, the sentence wraps and the columns line up.
7. Ordinary posts behave as before: reactions, comments, notifications and spoilers.

**#346: title actions**
1. The title page reads Rank · Bookmark · Recommend · Add to list. Each icon opens its own control.
2. TalkBack reads them in that order, and at the largest font size they stay on one row.

**#347: reviewed rankings join the group**
1. Seven first rankings make one carousel. A reviewed eighth, four minutes later, joins it with a "Review" label, and the review text is in the sheet.
2. Adding or editing a review creates no new post.
3. A lone reviewed ranking stays single, and one more than 30 minutes later starts a new post.
4. A reviewed rewatch keeps its own card.
5. Private and blocked viewers see nothing extra.

**#348: series reviews, on *Yu-Gi-Oh! Duel Monsters***
1. Reviews appears after Cast/Trailers and before Similar, with Write a review.
2. Write, edit and share a review. A second account sees it, and Helpful works.
3. Spoiler masking works.
4. An unranked show says "Rank to leave a review".
5. Season pages show only season reviews.
