## 1. Log another watch → ranking (≈2 min)

A ranked film ▸ ⋯ ▸ **Log another watch**. Set a date, pick someone under *Watched with*,
type a note ▸ **Save watch**. The sheet turns into **How was it?** with the three bands and
nothing selected — **no Re-check / Keep prompt**. Pick a band ▸ comparisons ▸ reveal.

- The reveal's placement line says **Moved from #X → #Y** or **Still #X**.
- Repeat once and close at *How was it?* instead: the watch count rises, the ranking does
  not move.

## 2. Watch History (≈2 min)

Title ▸ *Watched N times ›*.

- No *Add a past watch*, no *Log another watch*.
- Each row: date first, **(First watch)** on the earliest dated one, **#X of Y** on the
  right, then *With …* and the note under the watch that has them.
- One row per viewing — no stacked "Placed #…" lines. Do an *Update your rating* on the
  title and come back: still one row per viewing, the latest one's number changed.
- ✎ pencil opens an inline editor (date, watched with, note) — save a note change.
- Title page: one watch shows its date; two or more shows *Watched N times*.

## 3. Feed (≈1 min, easiest with a 2nd account following you)

After step 1 the Feed shows the rewatch post with a grey **2nd watch** line under the
genre/runtime line, and the **first** post keeps its old score. Only one new post.

## 4. Search (≈30 s)

Rank a film you have not ranked, then search for it: it shows its **score**, not the dashed
*Rank* badge.

## 5. Goals (≈30 s)

Profile ▸ goals: **the number loads** (no "Could not load your goals"), the bar agrees
with it, and a rewatch this year counts.

## 6. Collection ▸ Lists (≈1 min)

Collection title ▸ **Movies / TV / Lists**. Lists shows your lists in place, with no
Watched/Watchlist tabs. Leave on Lists, switch tabs away and back, and kill/reopen: it
comes back on Lists. Covers: an empty list shows the neutral glyph, 1–3 titles the first
poster full, 4+ the 2×2 mosaic.

## 7. A list's page (≈3 min)

- Metadata reads **`N/M watched · Only you · Updated today`**; no "You've seen" line.
- **Share list** is the primary button, **Add titles** secondary.
- Tap **Only you** in the metadata → *Edit list settings* (title, description, Numbered,
  privacy — no ordering). Change to *Anyone with the link* ▸ Save → dialog **Make this
  list link-only?** with a different body and **Make link-only**.
- ⋯ → *Edit list settings*, *Share*, *Delete list* only.
- Turn Numbered on. **Long-press a row, drag it** two places, drop: rows make room while
  dragging, the numbers renumber on drop, and the order survives leaving and reopening.
- A row's ⋯ → **Remove from list**.

## 8. Privacy **(2nd account)** (≈2 min) — NOT yet proven by a person

Link-only list: open the shared link on the second account → it opens, metadata says
*Anyone with the link*, no Share button, not on your profile shelf. Public: shows
*Public*, appears on the shelf. Only you: the link shows *List unavailable*. Viewer
progress counts the viewer's own watches; *Add N unseen to my Watchlist* adds to the
viewer's Watchlist.
